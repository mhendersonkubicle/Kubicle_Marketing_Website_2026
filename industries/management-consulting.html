What enterprise features ship by default for consulting firms? +

Bulk user provisioning via CSV or SCIM, SSO across SAML and OIDC, MFA on every administrator account, group hierarchies, role‑based access, white‑label branding and a Reporting API. Audit reports available under NDA.

Can Kubicle integrate with our existing LMS and tech stack? +

Yes. Off‑the‑shelf integrations with Cornerstone, Degreed, EdCast and Workday Learning, plus xAPI, an OData feed and a Reporting API for anything else. Custom integrations are built by our engineering team when the firm needs something bespoke.

How do we track learning impact across the practice? +

Engagement, completion and skill‑gain dashboards by cohort, persona and individual. Pre‑ and post‑program assessments quantify capability change. Scheduled CSV exports go to your data warehouse, or read directly from the Reporting API.

What support do you provide at scale? +

Each enterprise customer gets a dedicated onboarding specialist for setup and a customer success manager for the lifetime of the contract. Quarterly reviews keep the program calibrated to the next 12 months.

What content is covered for consulting? +

Excel, PowerPoint, SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau, Alteryx, RPA, AI Fundamentals, Machine Learning, Statistical Analysis, Financial Modeling, Data Ethics & Risk and Storytelling. New courses ship monthly.

Are credentials externally recognised? +

Yes. CPD, CPE and NASBA accredited. Every course completion contributes credits learners can apply to professional development requirements and post directly to LinkedIn.

Will it scale to global teams and rapid rollouts? +

From ten learners to ten thousand without re‑architecting. Multilingual content, multi‑region data residency available, SSO and SCIM mean adding a region is a configuration change, not a project.

How does it adapt to different teams and roles? +

Through Kubicle Paths. Build role‑calibrated journeys for analysts, associates, principals, partners and engagement managers. Save them as templates, clone for the next intake, version as the role evolves.

How quickly can we deploy? +

About a week for most firms. Sometimes a single day for teams with SSO and clean user data ready to go. Our customer success team handles the heavy lifting end‑to‑end.
