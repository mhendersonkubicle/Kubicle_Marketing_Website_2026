Who is this subject designed for? +

Finance and consulting professionals running iterative or change‑heavy projects. Useful for project leads, analysts, associates, managers, change leads and anyone delivering work where scope and priorities shift mid‑project. No prior Agile experience required to start.

How much time does it take? +

Roughly 6.5 hours of content across 6 courses and projects, worth 5.5 CPE/CPD credits. Most learners complete the full subject in two to three weeks of casual study.

Do I need a coding or technical background? +

No. Adaptive Project Management is a methods subject, not a software one. It's pitched at non‑technical professionals who run, contribute to or sponsor delivery work in a finance or consulting setting.

Should I take Project Management Fundamentals first? +

If you're new to project management entirely, the Project Management Fundamentals subject covers traditional methods first and is a useful primer. If you already work in a project setting and just need the Agile, hybrid and Lean toolkit, jump straight into this subject.

Does it cover both Agile and traditional? +

It focuses on the adaptive side: Agile, hybrid, Kanban, Scrum and Lean. Hybrid Approaches (course 3) covers when to mix Agile and traditional, and how to manage delivery when both are running side by side. For deeper traditional‑method coverage, pair this with Project Management Fundamentals.

What are the workplace projects? +

Three of the six items are applied workplace projects: Kanban in Practice, Scrum Methodology and Lean Thinking. Learners step through each method on a realistic scenario rather than just reading about the framework.

Are the credentials accredited? +

Yes. Kubicle credentials are independently accredited by CPD, CPE and NASBA. Every course awards CPE/CPD credits that learners can apply toward continuing professional development. Certificates are shareable on LinkedIn the moment they're issued.
