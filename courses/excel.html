Who is this subject designed for? +

Anyone in finance and consulting who works in Excel, which is essentially everyone in audit, advisory, deals, tax, FP&A, corporate finance and risk. The twenty‑nine courses and projects are pitched at every level, from complete beginners through to analysts moving up to advanced Power Pivot and DAX. No prior Excel experience required to start.

How much time does it take? +

Roughly thirty hours of content across fifteen courses and projects, worth 25.5 CPE/CPD credits. Most learners complete the full subject across six to ten weeks of casual study, picking the series that map to their role rather than running every item end to end.

Do I need an Excel background? +

No. Track 01 starts from data manipulation and basic formulas. Analysts already comfortable with Excel typically jump in at Lookups, Pivot Tables or the Modelling tracks; advanced users go straight to Macros, Power Pivot and DAX. The full subject runs end to end if a learner wants the complete picture.

Where does this fit in the wider Kubicle library? +

Excel is a single subject inside the wider Kubicle library, which also covers Power BI, Tableau, SQL, Visualization Fundamentals, Data Literacy, AI literacy, financial modelling and project management. Most finance and consulting teams start with Excel and then add Power BI, SQL or Visualization Fundamentals to extend the toolkit.

Are the credentials accredited? +

Yes. Kubicle credentials are independently accredited by CPD, CPE and NASBA. Every course and project awards CPE/CPD credits that learners can apply toward continuing professional development. Certificates are shareable on LinkedIn the moment they're issued.

Does this cover Power Pivot, Power Query and DAX? +

Yes, all three. Track 07 covers Power Pivot and Power Query end to end, including data modelling, Power Maps and the visualisation options that go beyond plain Pivot Tables. Track 08 covers DAX in Power Pivot, from essential functions through advanced time intelligence and an applied workplace project. Works with modern Excel (Microsoft 365 or Excel 2019 and later); Power Query is also available as Get and Transform in Excel 2016.
