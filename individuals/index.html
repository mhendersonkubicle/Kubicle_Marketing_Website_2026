Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Got a question about Kubicle? You’re in the right place. Whether you’re curious about our courses, pricing, certifications, or platform features, we’ve compiled the most common queries to help you get quick, clear answers. If you don’t find what you’re looking for, our support team is always here to help.

What is Kubicle’s Free Tier? +

Kubicle’s free tier is designed to help early-career professionals in finance and professional services build practical, job-ready skills. It gives you a chance to experience the platform and see how our learning approach works before committing. With the free tier, you get access to the first course in every professional learning path. Furthermore you gain the ability to explore every course in our library and preview the first lesson, and the opportunity to earn CPE and CPD credits for any free course exams you pass.

Why can’t I find any details about Kubicle’s Free Tier on their main website? +

Kubicle’s Free Tier is currently a beta offering. We are rolling it out gradually to a limited group of users so we can test the experience, gather feedback, and make improvements before a wider release. Because of this, you will not yet see full details of the Free Tier across our main website. If you have been invited to access it or have discovered it through limited advertising, you will see the relevant features available in your account. We plan to share more information publicly once the beta phase is complete.

What training does Kubicle provide? +

Kubicle offers practical, skills-based training designed to help professionals build confidence in the tools and techniques used in finance and professional services. Our courses focus on real-world applications of Excel, PowerPoint, data analysis, financial modeling, reporting, and problem solving. We combine clear explanations with hands-on exercises so you can learn by doing. Whether you are just starting out or looking to level up your technical skills, Kubicle helps you develop capabilities you can apply immediately in your role.

How do I upgrade? +

At the moment, upgrades are handled directly by our team. To move to a paid plan, please reach out to a Kubicle representative through our Talk to Sales page. A member of the team will help you choose the right plan and guide you through the next steps.

How do I enquire about Kubicle’s team training products? +

Please reach out to a Kubicle representative through our Talk to Sales page.

Is my training certified? +

Yes. Kubicle courses are eligible for Continuing Professional Education (CPE) and Continuing Professional Development (CPD) credits. Our training is recognized by bodies such as CAI Ireland and NASBA. You can earn credits by successfully completing course exams, and certificates are available to download directly from the platform.

Why does Kubicle ask for my phone number upon registration? +

We ask for a phone number to help verify accounts and ensure fair use of our Free Tier. This helps prevent the same individual from creating multiple accounts and taking advantage of the free offer, allowing us to keep the platform accessible and valuable for genuine users.
