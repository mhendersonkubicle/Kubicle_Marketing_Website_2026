The common organisational problem

Nobody starts
from the same place.

An organisation is not one skill level. It is hundreds of people reading the same dashboards very differently: some who model and query for a living, some who have never questioned a chart, and some quietly running the business from spreadsheets nobody can audit. Left unmapped, that unevenness is invisible, and it is where the risk and the wasted spend live.

Step one
What good looks like

Data fluency is a mix
of four personas.

Every organisation needs a predetermined distribution of data skills: a broad base of Data Citizens, a working layer of Data Associates, a small cohort of Data Wranglers and a handful of Data Analysts, in the proportions your strategy demands. The four personas are how you define that target, and what every person is measured against.
