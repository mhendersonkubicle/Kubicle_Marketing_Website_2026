Fall 2025 | Celebrating Our Latest G2 Awards

Kubicle News 17 Sep 2025 4 min read

This season, we’re proud to have earned recognition across multiple categories, including:

  • Best Relationship – for building trusted partnerships with customers.

  • Best Support – across Enterprise and Small-Business, recognizing our dedication to providing exceptional service.

  • Easiest To Do Business With – proving that Kubicle is not only powerful, but simple and seamless to work with.

  • High Performer – across Global, EMEA, Enterprise, Mid-Market, and Small-Business reports, showing our consistent satisfaction scores across regions and company sizes.

  • Leader – in both EMEA and Global Grid® Reports, highlighting strong market presence and customer satisfaction.

  • Momentum Leader – ranking in the top 25% of our category for innovation and growth.

  • Users Love Us – perhaps the one that makes us smile the most, because it’s earned after collecting 20+ glowing reviews with an average rating of 4.0 or higher.

What Our Customers Are Saying

These awards aren’t just badges - they reflect what our customers have been telling us. Reviewing feedback on G2, a few recurring themes stand out. They illustrate not only what we do well, but why these recognition badges feel so earned.

Ease of Use is one of the top things people mention. Users appreciate how intuitive and well-designed the platform is. Whether they’re beginners or more advanced, many say the user interface is clean, navigation is simple, and the learning paths make sense.

Outstanding Customer Support comes up again and again. When someone hits a snag, they tell us our support team is responsive, knowledgeable, and helpful. It’s clear from the reviews that having a team who listens, helps quickly, and guides users is something people really value. 

Practical, Real-World Learning is another strength. You tell us that Kubicle doesn’t waste time with excess theory. Lessons are concise, grounded in practical application, with hands-on projects and clear examples. Many users highlight how they can apply what they learn immediately in their jobs, whether it’s data analysis, Excel modelling, Power BI, or visualization.

Strong Educational Resources & Skill Development are also frequent compliments. Users appreciate a variety of lessons, the ability to learn at their own pace, and the structure provided by learning paths, quizzes, and assessments. These features help people build both confidence and actual, measurable skills.

Where We’re Listening and Improving

Of course, along with praise, you’ve also shared where we can do better. We believe that part of earning these awards is staying committed to growth.

Some of you have asked for more advanced content, with deeper topics and broader variety. Others have suggested enhancements to reporting features - especially for administrators who want more flexibility and insights. We’ve also heard comments on hands-on modules and administrative settings, where a smoother experience would make a real difference.

We see this feedback as an opportunity, and we’re excited to share that several new features and innovations are already in the pipeline. You’ll be seeing these roll out over the coming weeks and months. Stay tuned - it’s going to be a big end of year.

Why This Matters

These awards, together with your feedback, reinforce what we set out to build: a learning platform that is not only excellent in features and breadth, but also deeply attuned to what users need, what helps them do their work better, and what makes learning enjoyable and effective.

To our customers - thank you. Your trust and feedback power everything we do. 

To our team - you’ve done it again. Let’s keep pushing the boundary, listening, iterating, and raising the bar.
