What's the difference between a single academy and All Access? +

A single academy gives your team focused training on one discipline (AI, Data, Project Management, or Finance) at $175 per learner per year. All Access bundles all four academies plus 300+ courses for $499 per learner per year. It is the right choice when roles span more than one discipline.

How do volume discounts work? +

Discounts apply automatically based on the number of licenses you buy. The slider above shows the discount tier for any team size from 20 to 10,000+. Discounts are the same for single-academy and All Access plans.

Can we mix academies across teams? +

Yes. You can buy different academies for different teams, or combine single-academy plans with All Access licenses. We'll structure the contract to match how your organization is set up.

What about 10,000+ licenses or enterprise rollouts? +

For 10,000+ licenses we move to a custom contract with bespoke pricing, dedicated success management, and a phased deployment plan. Drag the slider to the top tier and we'll be in touch.

Do you offer monthly billing? +

All plans are 12-month licenses billed annually. Multi-year agreements are available for enterprise customers and unlock additional discounts.

Is there a free trial? +

Individuals can join for free at any time. For business teams we offer a guided pilot. Talk to us and we'll set you up with a sample cohort.
