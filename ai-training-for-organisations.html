What is corporate AI training? +

Corporate AI training is a structured program that builds practical AI skills across an organisation's workforce, from everyday tool fluency with ChatGPT and Copilot to AI strategy and governance for leaders. Unlike ad-hoc courses, a corporate program maps training to roles, measures skill gain, and reports outcomes to leadership.

How do you roll out AI training across a whole organisation? +

Kubicle runs a four-step managed rollout: discovery to align the program with your business goals, a workforce assessment that benchmarks every learner against four AI personas, program design that assigns role-calibrated learning paths, and customisation with your tools, policies and use cases. A dedicated customer success manager runs the program end to end.

How long does an organisation-wide AI training rollout take? +

The platform goes live in days, and most organisations complete benchmarking and launch their first cohorts within two to four weeks. Individual pathways run from roughly 8 to 13 guided hours depending on the persona, so an organisation typically sees its first measurable capability shift within the first quarter.

Does Kubicle's AI training satisfy the EU AI Act's AI literacy requirement? +

Article 4 of the EU AI Act requires organisations that provide or use AI systems to ensure a sufficient level of AI literacy in their staff. Kubicle's persona-based program is built to evidence exactly that: role-appropriate AI literacy training, completion records, assessments and audit-ready reporting across the workforce. See our EU AI Act training guide for the detail.

How is AI training for organisations different from AI training for employees? +

AI training for employees describes the learner-level pathways: the persona-aligned courses each person follows. AI training for organisations is the program wrapped around those pathways: workforce benchmarking, cohort design, governance alignment, customer success management and leadership reporting. Kubicle delivers both as one managed program; the employee view lives on our AI training for employees page.

How do you measure the impact of corporate AI training? +

Every learner is benchmarked before training, graded through AI-assessed projects during it, and tracked on completion and skill gain by cohort, team and region. Kubicle learners average an 80% course completion rate against a 7% industry benchmark, and reporting dashboards give leadership a live view of capability across the organisation.
