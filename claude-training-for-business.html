What is Claude training for business? +

Claude training for business teaches employees how to use Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant, productively and responsibly at work: navigating the platform, working across long and multiple documents, and building reusable workflows with Artifacts, Projects and Skills. Kubicle delivers it as a managed program: three accredited courses plus custom exercises and workplace tasks built around your own tools, data and scenarios.

What do the Claude courses cover? +

The program is three courses. Interface and Core Features orients a new user in the platform and its key features. Long Contexts and Documents focuses on document-heavy work: single and multi-document analysis, synthesis and Projects. Artifacts, Projects and Skills turns Claude from a chat tool into a tailored one. Together they form a beginner to intermediate progression, with Projects as the connective thread.

How is Kubicle's Claude training different from reading the docs? +

Generic documentation teaches Claude's features. Kubicle builds capability in the tool and then produces training and assets in the language of your organisation: context videos, applied exercises and guided workplace tasks tied to real scenarios in your business. That is how a learner knows exactly where to use Claude, so the training is implemented, not just completed.

Can you tailor the Claude program to our organisation? +

Yes. Alongside the three accredited courses, Kubicle produces custom context videos, exercises and workplace tasks mapped to your roles, tools, data and ways of working, and can help you shape Projects and Skills for your teams. The program is delivered through a managed process of discovery, scripting, production, configuration and testing, a pilot and a full launch, run by a dedicated customer success manager. See custom learning design for how we build bespoke assets.

Is our data safe when employees use Claude? +

The program is built around the business plans, Team and Enterprise, where your conversations and data are not used to train Anthropic's models by default. Learners are taught what data should never go into an AI assistant, where not to rely on Claude such as precise numerical work or authoritative legal records, and how to catch its failure modes and review every output before it is used or shared.

How long does it take to roll out the Claude program? +

The platform goes live in days. Custom assets and workplace tasks are scripted and produced over a few weeks, then configured and tested in a sandbox before a limited pilot and full launch. Most organisations move from discovery to a live pilot within a quarter. Explore the wider rollout in our AI training for organisations program.
