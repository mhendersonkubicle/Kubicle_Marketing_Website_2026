Discover your data profile

Kubicle's assessment will help you find your data persona.

Every professional sits somewhere on the data literacy ladder. The assessment places you on one of four personas, each with a clear definition of what they can do today and the skills they need to grow next.

Persona 01

Data Citizen

A Data and Digital Citizen understands the underlying concepts and theory associated with digital tools, data and the building of data projects. Data citizens may not be able to lead complex data projects, but they understand the key principles and can contribute to intermediate data project discourse. This level allows business leaders to participate in data and tech discussions without needing the execution skills of an analyst.

Start the Assessment →
Persona 02

Data Associate

A Data Associate is at the start of their data analysis journey with a core set of technical and analytical skills focused on no-code tools and basic data theory. They are proficient in spreadsheet and presentation tools, can perform simple database queries, and have a foundational understanding of statistics. They can transform raw data into actionable insights using accessible tools and critical thinking.

Start the Assessment →
Persona 03

Data Wrangler

The Data Wrangler can execute more complex data projects and is equipped to lead parts of a data project program. With high confidence, they clean, organise and transform raw data for analysts. While they have deep capabilities in core data technology, they are also building peripheral capabilities in other professional analyst tools.

Start the Assessment →
Persona 04

Data Analyst

A Kubicle Data Analyst is proficient in collecting, organising and interpreting information to make it useful to a range of businesses. They excel in complex data analysis, storytelling and championing data projects. The Data Analyst deep-dives into advanced techniques and tools: Statistical Analysis, Python Fundamentals, Machine Learning, Data Prep with SQL and Alteryx.

What the assessment actually measures.

Kubicle's data literacy framework focuses on the four essential skills every modern professional needs to work confidently with data. The assessment tests you across all four and shows you exactly where to focus next.

Pillar 01

Read Data

Recognise different types of data, where to find them, and how to evaluate their quality. Covers data access, data culture, and the legal and ethical responsibilities of using data.

Pillar 02

Work with Data

Practical skills to collect, clean, store and manage data: spreadsheets, tools, secure dataset handling and the internal data policies that keep teams compliant.

Pillar 03

Argue with Data

Explain insights clearly and persuasively. Assesses how well you communicate with visuals, build simple data stories and adapt your message to different audiences.

Pillar 04

Analyze Data

Use analytical techniques to interpret data and make decisions. Applies basic statistics, builds models and solves real business problems with data.
