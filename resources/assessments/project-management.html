Kubicle's assessment will help you find your project management persona.

Every project professional sits somewhere on the capability ladder. The assessment places you on one of three personas, each with a clear definition of what they can do today and the skills they need to grow next.

Persona 01

Project Member

The Project Member already contributes to projects in a specialist or functional capacity. They want to formalise their PM knowledge, understand the broader project context, and earn a credential that validates their competence, the foundation of any capable project team.
