Every finance professional sits somewhere on the capability ladder. The assessment places you on one of three personas, each with a clear definition of what they can do today and the skills they need to grow next.

Persona 01

Finance Apprentice

The Finance Apprentice is the Excel-literate finance contributor, confident with formulas, formatting and basic pivot work.
