What's included in All Access? +

All four Kubicle academies (AI, Data Literacy, Project Management and Finance), every persona pathway within them, and the full library of 300+ courses. You also get unified persona benchmarking that spans the academies, custom cross-discipline learning paths, and a dedicated customer success team.

Can different teams use different academies? +

Yes. The platform routes learners into the academies and persona pathways that fit their role. Your finance team can sit primarily in the Finance Academy; your delivery team in PM. The dashboards still report cleanly by team and by academy.

How does benchmarking work across academies? +

A single assessment maps each learner across all four academies, scoring them against the relevant capability axes. The output is a per-learner profile showing where they sit on every discipline that's relevant to their role, which then drives the recommended cross-discipline pathway.

Will it integrate with our LMS and SSO? +

In short, yes. We offer off-the-shelf integrations with a handful of leading LMS platforms, and our engineering team can build a custom integration for any other system you run. SSO is supported across the board, and a Reporting API is available for automated dashboards. Or run Kubicle standalone on our platform with full engagement and skill-gain reporting baked in.

Do learners see all four academies, or only what's relevant? +

Both work. By default learners see the full library so they can explore beyond their assigned pathways. If you'd rather scope visibility to specific academies for specific cohorts, we can configure that during rollout.

How is pricing structured? +

All Access is per-learner with volume discounts that increase as your rollout grows. Standard list is $499 per learner per year. Single-academy pricing starts at $175 per learner per year if you only need one. We'll send a tailored quote after the discovery call.
