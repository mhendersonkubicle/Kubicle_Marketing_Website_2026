WHY A PERSONA APPROACH?

AI training isn’t
one‑size‑fits‑all.
Calibration is.

Most AI programs teach a single tool to a mixed audience and hope it sticks. It rarely does. AI work spans everyday users, applied operators, technical builders, and organizational leaders, each with different decisions to own and different gaps to close.

What matters is the right depth at the right tier. Our four personas make that depth designable, so you teach foundational fluency to an Apprentice, applied productivity to a Navigator, build craft to an Architect, and strategic leadership to a Strategist, in one connected pathway.

UNDERSTANDING COMPETENCY CALIBRATION

Four core competencies.
Every persona, a different mix.

We benchmark every learner against the four competencies a serious AI capability demands, from foundational theory through to the strategy and governance work that keeps AI safe and aligned.

01

AI Theory & Foundations

Core concepts, terminology, generative AI, model behavior, and the principles every AI‑literate professional should share.

02

Tools & Platforms

ChatGPT, Copilot, agent platforms, prompt craft, workflow integration, and everyday productivity uplift across the team.

03

Architecture & Engineering

Custom AI components, agentic workflows, system integration, data plumbing, and production deployment of AI solutions.

04

Strategy & Governance

AI strategy, adoption management, ethics, regulatory landscape, risk and the governance posture that keeps deployment safe.

SHARED COMPETENCIES, DIFFERENT DEPTHS
