WHY A PERSONA APPROACH?

Project training isn’t
one‑size‑fits‑all.
Calibration is.

Most project programs teach a single method to a mixed audience and hope it sticks. It rarely does. Project work spans coordinators, delivery leads, and credentialed professionals, each with different decisions to own and different gaps to close.

What matters is the right depth at the right tier. Our three personas make that depth designable, so you teach foundational fluency to a project member, hands-on delivery craft to a PM candidate, and CAPM-grade rigor to a project champion, in one connected pathway.

UNDERSTANDING DOMAIN CALIBRATION

Four PMI domains.
Every persona, a different mix.

We benchmark every learner against the same four domains PMI tests on the CAPM exam, weighted exactly as PMI weights them. So progress on Kubicle is progress toward the credential.

01

PM Fundamentals & Core Concepts

36%

Lifecycles, roles, terminology, and the project environment: the shared language that keeps every project team on the same page.

02

Predictive Delivery

17%

Schedule, cost, quality, resources, risk, and integration in waterfall and structured delivery contexts.

03

Agile & Hybrid Delivery

20%

Scrum, Kanban, lean, and hybrid approaches that blend predictive structure with adaptive, iterative practice.

04

Business Analysis

27%

Stakeholder needs, requirements, research methods, and value alignment: the work that gives a project its purpose.

SHARED DOMAINS, DIFFERENT DEPTHS

Every persona is measured against the same four PMI domains. What differs is the depth across each, and that signature is what makes a persona fit for purpose.
