Finance Apprentice

Excel-literate finance contributor. Confident with formulas, formatting and basic pivot work. Understands the P&L, balance sheet and cash flow conceptually. Anchored to MOS Excel Associate (MO-201).

Which credentials does the program prepare learners for? +

Three external anchors, one per persona tier. Apprentice prepares for Microsoft Office Specialist Excel Associate (MO-201). Analyst prepares for MOS Excel Expert (MO-202), Microsoft PL-300 Power BI Data Analyst Associate, and Tableau Specialist. Modeler is calibrated to FMVA-comparable competencies, the closest external market reference for end-to-end financial modeling.

Can different finance roles learn together, or do we need separate cohorts? +

Both work. The three personas let you mix junior accountants, FP&A analysts and modeling specialists in a single program, each pulled into a pathway calibrated to their role and credential target. Hybrid cohorts are common, and the dashboards still report cleanly by persona.

Are Kubicle credentials accredited? +

Yes. Kubicle credentials are accredited by CPD, CPE and NASBA, so they apply toward continuing professional development credits for accountants and finance professionals. Certificates are shareable on LinkedIn the moment they're earned.

Will it integrate with our LMS and SSO? +

In short, yes. We offer off-the-shelf integrations with a handful of leading LMS platforms, and our engineering team can build a custom integration for any other system you run. SSO is supported across the board, and a Reporting API is available for automated dashboards. Or run Kubicle standalone on our platform with full engagement and skill-gain reporting baked in.

How does the content stay current with Excel and Power BI updates? +

Modules are continuously refreshed by our in-house learning design team to reflect new Excel features, Power BI releases, evolving Microsoft and Tableau certification objectives, and changes to financial modeling best practice. You're never paying for material that's gone stale.

How is pricing structured? +

Pricing is per-license with volume discounts that increase as your rollout grows. The Finance Program starts at $175 per learner; Kubicle All Access starts at $499 per learner per year. We'll send a tailored quote after the discovery call.
