WHY A PERSONA APPROACH?

Finance training isn’t
one‑size‑fits‑all.
Calibration is.

Most finance training teaches Excel to a mixed audience and hopes it sticks. It rarely does. Finance work spans junior contributors, FP&A analysts, and modeling specialists, each with different decisions to own and different gaps to close.

What matters is the right depth at the right tier. Our three personas make that depth designable, so you teach foundational fluency to a finance apprentice, dashboard and FP&A craft to an analyst, and FMVA‑grade modeling to a modeler, in one connected pathway.
