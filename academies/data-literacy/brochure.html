WHY A PERSONA APPROACH?

Data fluency isn’t
a single skill set.
It’s a balance of four.

Most data literacy programs teach one tool to a mixed audience and hope it sticks. It rarely does. A board director needs to read and challenge data; a wrangler needs to clean and transform it. The same syllabus cannot serve both.

What matters is the right depth at the right tier. Our four personas make that depth designable, so you teach foundational fluency to a Citizen, applied craft to an Associate, technical build skill to a Wrangler, and deep specialism to an Analyst, in one connected pathway.

UNDERSTANDING CAPABILITY CALIBRATION

Four capability axes.
Every persona, a different mix.

We benchmark every learner across the four capabilities a data‑fluent professional needs: from the technical craft of working with data, to the strategic craft of arguing with it.

01

Working with Data

Collecting, cleaning, blending and transforming raw data with SQL, BI tools and automation platforms so the rest of the business can move.

02

Reading Data

Interpreting charts, statistics and reports with confidence. Understanding what’s being said, what isn’t, and what to ask next.

03

Analyzing Data

Statistics, modeling, and machine learning that turn complex datasets into structured insight and forward‑looking predictions.

04

Arguing with Data

Storytelling, presenting and challenging data with rigour. Turning numbers into evidence‑based decisions that actually land.

SHARED CAPABILITIES, DIFFERENT DEPTHS

Every persona is measured against the same four capabilities. What differs is the depth across each, and that signature is what makes a persona fit for purpose.
