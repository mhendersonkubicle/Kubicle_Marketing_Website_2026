The Kubicle Data Persona Framework

Four data personas.
Mapped against four capability axes.
Calibrated to your team.

Data fluency isn't a single skill set. It's a balance of four. A board director needs to read and challenge data; a data wrangler needs to clean and transform it. Our framework benchmarks every learner across Working with, Reading, Arguing with and Analyzing data, then assigns the right pathway.

Download the Data Persona Brochure (PDF)

Data Citizen

Reads and challenges data with confidence. Understands core concepts well enough to ask the right questions in any meeting, without needing to run the analysis themselves.

How quickly can we launch? +

Most clients are live with learners in their first lesson inside about a week, sometimes inside a single day for teams with SSO and clean user data ready to go. Our customer success team handles setup end-to-end.

Can different roles learn together, or do we need separate cohorts? +

Both work. Our four data personas let you mix C-suite, managers, analysts and frontline staff in a single program, each pulled into a pathway calibrated to their role and depth. Hybrid cohorts are common, and the dashboards still report cleanly by persona.

Are the credentials accredited? +

Yes. Kubicle credentials are accredited by CPD, CPE and NASBA, so learners can apply them toward continuing professional development credits. Certificates are shareable on LinkedIn the moment they're earned.

Will it integrate with our LMS and SSO? +

In short, yes. We offer off-the-shelf integrations with a handful of leading LMS platforms, and our engineering team can build a custom integration for any other system you run. SSO is supported across the board, and a Reporting API is available for automated dashboards. Or run Kubicle standalone on our platform with full engagement and skill-gain reporting baked in.

How is the content kept current? +

Modules are continuously refreshed by our in-house learning design team to reflect new tools, evolving regulation and the rise of generative AI. You're never paying for material that's gone stale.

How is pricing structured? +

Pricing is per-license with volume discounts that increase as your rollout grows. The Data Literacy Program starts at $175 per learner; Kubicle All Access starts at $499 per learner per year. We'll send a tailored quote after the discovery call.
